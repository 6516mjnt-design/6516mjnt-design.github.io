History of AI and Scientific Development

  • 1920s: 1925 — Heisenberg's matrix mechanics / 1926 — Schrödinger's wave equation
  • 1940s–50s: 1943 — Schrödinger's "What Is Life?" (coined the term "aperiodic crystal" for the structure of DNA) / 1953 — Discovery of the DNA double helix (influenced by "What Is Life?")
  • 1950s–70s (1st AI boom): 1956 — Dartmouth Conference (the concept of AI was established) / 1957 — Perceptron model
  • 1980s–90s (2nd AI boom): 1982 — Hopfield network (John Hopfield) / 1986 — Backpropagation
  • 2000s–10s (3rd AI boom): 2006 — Deep learning (Geoffrey Hinton) / 2015 — AlphaGo
  • 2017: Google researchers introduced the "Transformer" neural network architecture (paper: "Attention Is All You Need")
  • 2020: OpenAI researchers published "scaling laws" (paper: "Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models")
  • 2020s– (4th AI boom): The era of generative and personalized AI

Notes

*1 In China, DeepSeek (深度求索) released R1 in January 2025, an event dubbed the "DeepSeek Shock" at the time. Z.ai then released GLM-5.2 on June 17, 2026. Moonshot AI (月之暗面) released Kimi-K3 on July 17; on some benchmark tests Kimi-K3 reportedly scored higher than GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5. Alibaba announced Qwen3.8 on July 18. Note that all Chinese-made models listed here are open-weight, while the models from the three major US companies are closed.

*2 The 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics (for the "machine learning" underlying AI) was awarded to John Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton. The Nobel Prize in Chemistry (for AI-based "protein structure prediction and design") was awarded to Demis Hassabis, John Jumper, and David Baker.

*3 Claude Mythos 5 and Fable 5 were released simultaneously on June 9, 2026 (US time); Fable is the "publicly available version" of Mythos. On June 12, 2026 (US time), the US government issued export control directives, suspending access for all users. On August 7, OpenAI announced that it could not rule out that its next-generation model, Astra, might reach "Critical," the highest tier under its safety guidelines, and paused part of its development.

*4 For a more comprehensive look at other AI models, Poe is a useful reference: https://poe.com/
